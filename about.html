About the Artist

YOUNESS EL aka YOUNES.ART is a self taught painter born in 1993 based in MARRAKECH, MOROCCO. He has been painting since late 2017, using paints to present unique conceptual ideas, inspired by the human emotions we experience and the energy around us.

YOUNES.ART has found solace in creating paintings and pursuing his true passion in art. He has sold paintings to customers around the world, and exhibited his vibrant paintings at multiple exhibitions as an up-and-coming artist.
